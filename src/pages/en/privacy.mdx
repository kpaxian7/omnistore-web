OmniStore is a local-first desktop app. This page describes what we collect, what we don't, and how we handle store credentials.

## What we collect

- **Account**: only when you subscribe to Pro — Stripe handles payment; our server only stores your email and subscription state.
- **Anonymous usage**: aggregate page metrics via Plausible. No personal identifiers.

## What we don't collect

- Any metadata, screenshots or descriptions edited inside the OmniStore desktop app.
- Google or Apple credentials — they are encrypted by the OS safeStorage and **never leave your machine**.
